Delete Account

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Delete from the app

Open Global Social Gym, sign in, go to Settings > Delete account, type DELETE, and confirm. The app verifies your logged-in session before deleting your authentication account.

What is deleted

Your profile, practice sessions, messages, feedback, reports, blocks, product interaction events, and translation quality logs are deleted where they are linked to your account by the database model.

What may remain

Aggregated server translation usage buckets do not identify a user and may remain. Store review records and infrastructure logs may remain for a limited period according to platform and hosting operations.

If you cannot access the app

If you cannot access the app, request account deletion by emailing 5jinoo@naver.com from the email address used for your Global Social Gym account.
