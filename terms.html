Global Social Gym

Terms of Use

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Purpose

Global Social Gym is for short social practice sessions. It is not for dating, therapy, medical advice, crisis support, hiring, lending, financial advice, or legal advice.

User conduct

Do not share external contact details, links, addresses, sexual content, threats, harassment, scam attempts, or requests that pressure another user. The app may block messages that violate these rules.

Reports and blocks

Users can report or block a practice partner. Reported sessions may be closed and blocked users are excluded from future matching with the reporting user.

No guarantee

The app may be unavailable, translations may be inaccurate, and practice partners may leave a session. Use the original message view when translation quality matters.

Account deletion

You may delete your account from Settings > Delete account. Account deletion is intended to be permanent.
