Global Social Gym

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

What the app does

Global Social Gym helps people practice short, safe social interactions with other global users. The app is not a dating service, therapy service, medical service, or emergency support service.

Account data

The app stores your account identifier, email sign-in state, nickname, primary language, practice goal, weekly goal, and reminder preferences so it can provide your profile and practice flow.

Practice data

The app stores original practice messages, practice sessions, feedback, social rep history, reports, and blocks. Original messages are stored so both participants can complete the practice session and so reported content can be reviewed.

Translation

The app tries to translate messages on your device first using operating system translation tools. Server translation fallback is optional, budget-limited, and off by default. Translation API keys are never stored in the app.

Safety

Reports and blocks are stored to reduce repeated unsafe matching. Practice messages are limited and checked for links, contact details, harassment, sexual content, scams, and violent wording before they are stored.

Account deletion

You can delete your account from Settings > Delete account. Deleting your account removes your authentication account and cascades deletion through your profile, sessions, messages, feedback, reports, blocks, and translation quality logs where the database model links that data to your account.

Contact

For privacy, safety, or account questions, contact 5jinoo@naver.com.
